ClearView

Understand a picture

Choose a picture or take one with your camera. ClearView will create a plain-language caption on this device. You can listen to it with speech.

About accuracy: AI can miss details or mistake what is in a picture. Use this description as a helpful guide, not as proof of identity, safety, or what an object is.

1. Add a picture

Choose an image from your device

You can also drag an image into this box or paste a copied image anywhere on this page. Supported formats depend on your browser.

Your photo is processed in this browser and is not uploaded to an AI service. The first time you describe an image, the app downloads the model files from Hugging Face. After that, your browser may keep them in its cache.

2. Get a description

The first run can take a few minutes while the free model is downloaded. Later runs are usually faster. Image understanding runs on your device; no account or API key is needed.

The richer model can give fuller captions, but its first download is about 1.5 GB and needs WebGPU. It is selected on supported browsers; other browsers start with the compact model. Choose the compact model if the richer one is too slow or unavailable.

Choose an image to begin.

3. Description

Speech uses the voices available on your device. If speech is unavailable, you can still read or copy the description.

Optional: read text in the picture

This separate tool looks for printed text, such as a sign or label. It runs in your browser and may misread small or blurry writing.

Choose an image to enable text reading.

ClearView runs image models locally. AI descriptions and text recognition can make mistakes; no current image model can promise complete accuracy.
